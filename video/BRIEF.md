## Intent and references

Prove that a film can retain the approved Within reach material, colours, expressive hands and angular silhouettes while guiding attention through time. Begin with the company/owner composition, move one symbolic payment, then widen the view to the latest older-owner figure. The art remains the anchor; the uploaded kit contributes beat planning, frame-based production and paper staging.

Reference: `explorations/style-frames/assets/within-reach.png`, the original company/owner/payment layers, and the latest salary/dividend scene plus its older-owner asset. No new images were generated, source art modified, or narration purchased. The initial format is a 1280×720, 30 fps, 9-second silent MP4. This is not a permanent choice of channel or aspect ratio.

## Visual argument and scope

An action today can be followed into a later chapter. The payment remains in the present owner's hand; the later figure holds a blank record with a temporal label. The frame does not define a financial outcome. Cash, paper and decorative notes are symbolic and have no numerical scale.

There is no salary/dividend selection, contribution transfer, pension balance, tax rate or quantified claim in this study. The original numerical-source blocker remains relevant to future financial films. A film on a specific financial choice must research its material consequences rather than reuse this generic transition as the full explanation.
