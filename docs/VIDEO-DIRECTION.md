# Within reach, in motion

This is the video direction for `codex-video`. The user wants to explore authored videos beyond the interactive-illustration format, while carrying forward the full visual identity developed on `codex`. The [supplied motion kit](../vendor/motion-kit/README.md) adds filmmaking and production techniques. It does not restart the design process.

## Reference hierarchy

1. The user's current request and explicitly approved **Within reach** direction.
2. The actual [master frame](../explorations/style-frames/assets/within-reach.png), [drawing system](STYLE-SYSTEM.md), [colour tokens](../design/within-reach/style-tokens.json), and protected production layers.
3. The [latest connected-effects scene](../explorations/salary-or-dividend/README.md), including its older owner, paper record, prompt provenance and temporal relationships. These extend the working vocabulary; they are not a claim that every lesson mechanism has received final approval.
4. This video direction, the [video workflow](VIDEO-WORKFLOW.md) and [editorial playbook](VIDEO-EDITORIAL.md). The user responded positively to the first motion study; use it as the current motion reference.
5. Selected techniques from the uploaded kit, adapted to the above.

The [video reference manifest](../design/within-reach/video-reference-manifest.json) records the actual files and hashes. Inspect the master, relevant components and latest scene before designing. The original **The colossus** and **Separate bodies** frames remain preserved alternatives, not ingredients to blend into this style.

## What must survive the change of medium

The film should feel made from the same paper as the illustration: dense dyed fibres, compressed creases, scuffed ink, irregular angular edges, exaggerated hands and expressive silhouettes. Lemon `#ffdf08` holds the scene; ultramarine `#1244bb` supplies structural masses; orange `#f47b0d` identifies fictional cash; ink `#151819` and ivory `#fff9d8` carry the figures and paper. Preserve tonal variation in the original pixels. Keep depth shallow and space editorial rather than constructing a realistic world.

Reuse the approved raster components when they fit. Keep their original alignment grids and transparency. Move a cutout as a cutout; do not bend a single raster arm into rubber or stretch a character to fake a new pose. A new gesture or separated hand needs a reference-led asset with saved provenance, using [the existing image prompt](templates/IMAGE-PROMPT.md). Texture travels with the object. Added grain must not obscure the material or crawl across a still face. Do not automatically halftone, recolour, add white stickers around, or “clean” the approved assets.

## Let time do the teaching

The viewer's attention replaces the interaction's click as the pacing mechanism. An authored choice initiates a visible action; its connected consequences unfold in a deliberate order. Carry the same person, object or relationship through the film so a cut does not require the viewer to learn a new scene.

- Open on a meaningful image, not an empty title card. Establish who and what matters before introducing a second effect.
- Let one dominant event lead attention at a time. Anticipation, a short move and a readable settling moment give paper weight. Use springs with restraint; every object need not bounce.
- Reframe to expose a relationship, follow an action or reveal a later consequence. A camera move needs an explanatory purpose. Start with the master’s shallow editorial space; avoid ornamental 3D fly-throughs.
- Prefer an object continuing across a cut, a paper reveal, an occlusion or a controlled change of framing. Torn wipes are available, not mandatory punctuation between every beat.
- Hold long enough to understand a changed state. Do not inherit the kit's requirement for constant visible change or compulsory 12 fps jitter. A final settled image is part of the explanation.
- Make “later” visible through context, a short label, a date or a changed subject. Editing order alone does not establish financial time.

This is a visual grammar, not a repeated company-to-owner template. Each financial mechanism needs its own researched visual explanation. Buildings, characters, records and money earn their place through what they explain. The assistant originates that explanation.

## Connected consequences, with precise meanings

Carry forward [the causal principles](INTERACTION-PRINCIPLES.md): distinguish spendable money, tax liabilities, contribution room, records, eligibility and eventual benefits. Show existing rights persisting when an action adds nothing new. A future paper record is not a savings balance; a contribution is not a dollar-for-dollar pension entitlement.

Quantification belongs beside the thing it measures. If an object's count, size or area encodes value, define and verify the scale. Existing decorative notes inside the company do not encode a computed balance. Keep amounts and changing labels in code rather than generated pixels. Numerical claims still need checked primary sources, assumptions and calculations; the video medium does not remove the current source-verification blocker.

## Text, sound and format

Use quiet, readable sans-serif type with the illustration carrying the personality. Adapt type size to the delivery frame and check it at actual phone display size. Short labels belong near their subjects; narration can carry connective detail. Do not convert the film into paragraph cards or a moving dashboard. Prepare captions/transcripts for spoken films and a short descriptive account for silent studies.

Sound follows the material and the event: a paper contact, fold or restrained emphasis where it helps perception. Avoid a sound on every entrance and obligatory background music. Voice is optional and must use an actually available, authorized provider or supplied audio. Preserve supplied narration unless editing it is in scope. Test timing, intelligibility, clipping and caption accuracy when sound is introduced.

The first calibration is landscape, 1280×720 at 30 fps, matching the existing wide relationship. This is a practical test setting, not a permanent channel strategy. Vertical and square films need deliberate recomposition, readable hands/faces/labels, and space for platform controls and captions; do not blindly crop the landscape master.

## Visual acceptance

Compare real rendered opening, action, transition and settled frames against the master and latest figure. Check paper texture, figure identity, palette, transparency, hand/face readability, uncut silhouettes, text placement, causal continuity and explicit timing. Then inspect temporal continuity, not just isolated attractive stills. Record what is verified and what remains a creative judgment. A successful render is not user approval of a new visual direction or proof of financial correctness.
