A Canadian company and its individual shareholder. Symbolic notes show no amount or tax rate. Educational illustration, not financial advice.

What the illustration assumes

You own shares in the corporation; the corporation owns its cash. This example assumes a lawfully declared taxable dividend from after-tax company funds to an individual shareholder. The dividend is included in personal income using the applicable gross-up; dividend tax credits help recognise corporate tax already paid. The result depends on dividend type, province and personal circumstances. Salary, loans, returns of capital and capital dividends follow different rules. Moving the illustrations reveals grouping; it does not itself transfer assets.
