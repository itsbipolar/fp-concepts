Assumptions & sources

A qualitative Canadian illustration. The owner is 40, salary stays below the annual CPP ceiling, and there is no other earned income or employer pension plan. Notes, marks and paper sizes do not encode amounts. It does not compare equal company costs or calculate take-home pay. Actual results depend on earnings, limits, province and personal circumstances. Salary must be reasonable to be deductible. Earlier CPP history and existing RRSP room persist in either choice.

CPP is a record of pensionable earnings and pension rights, not a personal savings pot. RRSP room is permission to contribute, not cash deposited.

Rule references: CPP contributions, employer CPP responsibilities, RRSP contributions and room, and taxable Canadian dividends. Reference pages were inaccessible during this build; no current rates or amounts are asserted.
