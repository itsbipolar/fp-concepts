# Three ways to picture ownership

One company. One owner. Money held inside the company. Three art directions to review before building another interactive lesson.

**[Download the offline review (ZIP)](https://github.com/itsbipolar/fp-concepts/raw/refs/heads/codex/explorations/style-frames/style-frames.zip)** — extract it and open **FP Concepts - Style Frames.html** in a browser. All images are embedded; no server or installation is needed.

The images also display directly below on GitHub. Click an image to inspect it at full size.

## The colossus

Woodcut and screenprint. The owner is small; the institution is enormous. Scale makes their separation visible.

![The colossus: tiny owner beside a vermilion company holding yellow illustrated notes](assets/colossus.png)

## Separate bodies

Sculpture and hard light. Distinct forms on shared ground; money occupies the company’s space.

![Separate bodies: cobalt company holding pale green notes beside a coral sculptural owner](assets/separate-bodies.png)

## Within reach

Cut paper and expressive character. The owner can lean on the company. Their open hand is still empty.

![Within reach: expressive owner leaning on a blue company holding orange illustrated notes](assets/within-reach.png)

## Scope and provenance

These are still style frames created with OpenAI image generation, directed and revised for this brief. They are not yet interactive financial lessons or layered animation assets. The fictional notes imply no denomination or tax amount. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for intentions, risks and the next production step.

A pinned, Apache-2.0 licensed copy of [Anthropic’s frontend-design skill](../../.agents/skills/frontend-design/UPSTREAM.md) is installed under `.agents/skills/frontend-design/`. It guides the quiet review page, responsive layout and critique process. The artwork carries the visual identity.

## Files and checks

- `index.html`: normal gallery with relative PNG assets.
- `review.html`: gallery with all images embedded for offline use.
- `style-frames.zip`: downloadable review HTML and opening instructions.
- `assets/`: final generated PNGs.
- `preview.png`: browser screenshot of the comparison page.
- `package_review.py`: rebuilds the standalone HTML and ZIP after gallery or asset changes.

Verified in Chromium: image loading, mobile layout, enlarged viewer, keyboard controls, offline embedded images and ZIP integrity. No JavaScript errors. The images must still be judged by the owner before selecting a direction.
