# Company, owner, money: three style frames

The owner has rejected gardens as metaphors, numerical charts, overlapping amounts, literal streetscapes and disconnected abstract objects. The current approved scope is three still art-direction frames before another interactive lesson.

## Shared composition and idea

A simplified company holds recognisable money. A distinctive human owner stands beside it. The relationship, rather than architectural detail, is the subject. No tax mechanism yet. The viewer should read: “I own the company; the money is inside it.” The still is a style test, not a complete explanation of corporate ownership.

## Three authored treatments

- **The colossus:** flat vermilion woodcut, tiny confident black-ink owner, yellow notes, ivory paper. Scale makes the company a separate institution. Risk: feels imposing or political.
- **Separate bodies:** rigid cobalt company, coral collectible character, lime notes, lilac stage. Hard light and distinct bodies make separation tangible. Risk: the company may read as a safe; animation would need to establish it explicitly.
- **Within reach:** ultramarine paper company, exaggerated black-and-ivory owner, orange illustrated notes, lemon background. The owner leans on the company while their other hand is empty. The pose carries the idea. Risk: the open hand could be read as asking for money; interaction should resolve that ambiguity.

## Review-page plan

The artwork is the memorable element. The review page is quiet, neutral and deliberately minimal.

Palette: slate-white #f0f1f4, white #ffffff, ink #202737, secondary #626a78, border #d3d8e0, focus blue #285cbb.
Type: system sans for review labels and controls; no display typography competing with the images. Body 16px, captions 14px, headings 26px. Sentence case, no numbered art directions.
Layout: three equal images in a horizontal comparison; one column on narrow screens. Clicking an image opens a large accessible viewer with Previous/Next controls. Captions remain short and useful. No entrance animation or UI cards decorated with shadows.

## Provenance and scope

Images are generated with OpenAI image generation from original detailed art direction, then edited to remove real currency details. They are raster style frames, not layered animation assets. A selected direction needs independent vector/layered production artwork, a specific teaching interaction, and financial review before a finished lesson.
