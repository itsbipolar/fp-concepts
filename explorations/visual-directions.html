Visual directions: one rule, four ways to draw it

The rule: above $95,323 of net income (2026), 15 cents of OAS goes back for every dollar, until it is gone at $155,320. TFSA withdrawals don't count as income. Same retiree as Fig. 01 (net income $91,600 before any extra withdrawal). Move the slider and switch the source: all four sketches update together.



AThe line

Income as a length on a ruler; the threshold is a mark; what's past it is shaded. OAS is a row of 18 tiles that grey out.

BEvery dot is $1,000

A unit grid you can count. Dots past the line turn orange; about every 7 orange dots cost one gold OAS dot.

CThe shape of the rule

The rule drawn as its own shape: flat, a bend at $95,323, a slope down to zero. You are the dot riding it.

DTwo doors

No diagram at all: the same money through two doors, side by side, with the numbers doing the work.
